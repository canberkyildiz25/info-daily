Logitech's Mobi Fold is the company's first foldable mouse: it closes like a clamshell into a rectangle you can lose in a jacket pocket, and opens into something you can actually use for a working day. It launched in 2026 at $79.99.

This is a summary of the manufacturer's published specifications and the findings of reviewers who have used the device. We have not tested it ourselves, so nothing below is presented as first-hand experience — where a claim comes from a review, it is attributed.

## What Logitech Confirms

From [Logitech's product page](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/shop/p/mobi-fold-mouse) and the [launch announcement](https://ir.logitech.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Logitech-Launches-Mobi-Fold-Its-First-Ultra-Portable-Foldable-Mouse-for-Life-On-the-Go/default.aspx):

- **Price:** $79.99. A business version with a USB receiver is $89.99.
- **Sensor:** 4K DPI.
- **Connectivity:** Bluetooth, with Easy-Switch for moving between paired devices.
- **Compatibility:** Windows, macOS, ChromeOS, Android and iPadOS.
- **Scrolling:** a touch surface Logitech calls Adaptive Touch Scroll, rather than a physical wheel.
- **Power:** folding it switches it off; unfolding switches it on.
- **Handedness:** ambidextrous.
- **Colours:** Graphite, Off White, Lilac and Sand.
- **Durability claim:** rated by Logitech for 15 years of daily use, and tested to 50,000 fold cycles under controlled conditions.
- **Ergonomics claim:** Logitech cites an internal study finding 22% less forearm muscle strain than using a laptop trackpad. That figure is the manufacturer's own, not independent.

## The Numbers Reviewers Measured

[Techlicious](https://www.techlicious.com/blog/logitech-mobi-fold-foldable-travel-mouse/) and [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2190943/logitech-mobi-fold-review/) both put hands on the device:

- **Folded size:** roughly 2.2 × 2.5 inches, under an inch thick.
- **Weight:** a little over two ounces.
- **Battery:** up to about 30 days per charge, with one minute of charging reported to add around 22 hours of use.
- **Drop resistance:** to 90 cm.

## Where Reviewers Push Back on the Pitch

This is the part marketing copy leaves out, and it is the reason to read a review before buying.

**Ergonomics are the trade-off.** Engadget found the geometric shape gives your thumb and fingers nowhere natural to rest. A mouse that folds flat cannot also be contoured, and reviewers treat that as the price of the format rather than a defect.

**The touch scroll divides opinion.** Engadget reports it is not as smooth as a physical scroll wheel. If you scroll long documents or spreadsheets all day, this is the specification to weigh most carefully.

**The receiver costs extra.** If you need a USB dongle rather than Bluetooth — for a machine with restricted Bluetooth, or a KVM setup — that is the $89.99 business model, not the standard one.

**The price sits above the category.** At $79.99 it is priced against full-size productivity mice, and Engadget's conclusion was that the MX Anywhere 3S remains the better mouse for anyone willing to give up the fold.

Engadget scored it 8.4 out of 10 and called it one of the most travel-friendly pointers available.

## Who It Appears to Suit

Based on those findings, the Mobi Fold is aimed narrowly and hits that target:

- People who carry a laptop daily and currently use the trackpad because a mouse is one more thing to pack.
- Travellers working from trains, airport seats and hotel desks, where surface space is the constraint.
- Anyone who has bought a small travel mouse before and stopped using it because it cramped their hand — the fold exists to solve exactly that, though reviewers say it solves it partially.

It is a poor fit if you work mostly at one desk, if you scroll heavily, or if you want a gaming mouse. In those cases the format is asking you to pay for portability you will not use.

## The Short Version

The Mobi Fold does something genuinely new at a price that assumes you value it. Reviewers agree it is among the most portable mice you can buy and agree it is less comfortable than a conventional one. Whether that trade is worth $79.99 depends entirely on how often the mouse is in a bag rather than on a desk.
