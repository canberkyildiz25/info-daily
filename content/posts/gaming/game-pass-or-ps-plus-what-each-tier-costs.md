If you own a console, you don't really choose between these two: an Xbox gets Game Pass and a PS5 gets PlayStation Plus. The real decision is which tier to pay for, or whether to pay at all. With [consoles this expensive](/gaming/consoles-have-never-cost-more-make-yours-last), it is worth checking what you are getting.

The prices below come from [Microsoft's Game Pass page](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-game-pass) and [Sony's PlayStation Plus page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/ps-plus/), both read on 30 October 2026. They are US prices and can change.

## Xbox Game Pass

| Plan | Per month | Games | Key extras |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | $9.99 | 50+ | Online multiplayer, 5 hours of cloud play a month |
| Premium | $14.99 | 200+ | New Xbox-published games within a year of launch, 10 hours of cloud play |
| Ultimate | $22.99 | 500+ | New games on day one, EA Play, Fortnite Crew, Ubisoft+ Classics, 15 hours of cloud play |
| PC only | $13.99 | 300+ | PC only, new games on day one, EA Play |

All four are monthly on Microsoft's page, and it does not list an annual plan. Ultimate works out at about $276 a year. It has moved around: it [rose to $29.99 last October and fell to $22.99 in April](https://www.forbes.com/sites/antoniopequenoiv/2026/04/21/xbox-slashes-game-pass-ultimate-price-by-7-after-october-price-hike/). Forbes reported at the time that new Call of Duty games would no longer arrive on launch day, so check the current list if that series is your reason for subscribing.

## PlayStation Plus

| Plan | 1 month | 3 months | 12 months | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | $10.99 | $27.99 | $79.99 | Monthly games, online multiplayer, cloud saves, discounts |
| Extra | $16.99 | $43.99 | $134.99 | The Game Catalog of hundreds of games, Ubisoft+ Classics |
| Premium | $19.99 | $54.99 | $159.99 | Classics Catalog, game trials, PS5 streaming, Sony Pictures Catalog |

Sony [raised the 1-month and 3-month prices in May](https://www.nme.com/news/gaming-news/playstation-plus-essential-premium-price-hike-3947485). The 12-month plans change the sums a great deal. Essential drops to about $6.67 a month, Extra to $11.25 and Premium to $13.33. If you know you will keep a tier for a year, the monthly price is the expensive way to buy it.

## When Each Tier Makes Sense

**You only play online with friends.** The cheapest tier on either side is enough. Both include online console multiplayer.

**You play a few big games a year.** You may not need a catalogue at all. Add up what you would have bought last year and compare it with the annual cost of the tier. A library you don't get round to playing is not a saving.

**You get through a lot of games, or you share a console with children.** This is where Game Pass Premium or Ultimate and PS Plus Extra pay off, since one subscription can replace several purchases.

**You want new releases on the day they come out.** On Xbox, that is Ultimate. Microsoft's page says Premium gets new Xbox-published games within 12 months of launch.

**You like older PlayStation games.** The Classics Catalog is Premium only. If you don't care about classics, trials or streaming, Extra gets you the main library for less.

## Three Checks Before You Pay

- **Look at the catalogue first.** Both companies add and remove games, and Microsoft's terms say titles vary by plan, region and platform. Search for the games you actually want.
- **Watch the renewal.** Microsoft states that subscriptions continue automatically at the regular price unless cancelled. Microsoft's page currently shows a first 14 days for $1, with offers depending on your account. Put a reminder in your calendar if you take one.
- **Remember the cloud hours are capped on Xbox.** The monthly limits above are small if streaming is your main way to play.

## Start Cheap and Move Up

The cheapest tier is enough for online play. Start there, or with a single month of a higher tier, and see how many of its games you actually open. Move to an annual plan only once you know you'll use it for the whole year.
